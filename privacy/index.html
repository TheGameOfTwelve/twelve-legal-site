Privacy Policy

Draft version 1.0 · Prepared 27 November 2026 · Intended for the private beta of the Twelve mobile app

Draft for review. This notice has not been published as a final legal policy. It must be checked against the live service configuration before publication.

1. Who we are

The Game of Twelve® is a handcrafted tabletop game with a companion mobile application called Twelve. The application is operated by Nuno Brito, based in the United Kingdom. For privacy questions or requests, email thegameoftwelve@gmail.com.

This notice explains how personal information is processed when you register for and use the Twelve app, take part in its private beta, or contact us about it. It concerns the companion application, not separate purchases of physical boards.

2. Information we process

Accounts and identity

  • First name, surname, username and email address; selected avatar or profile picture where supported.
  • Account verification and authentication records, password-reset and email-change events, and session-related information. Authentication passwords are handled by the authentication service rather than disclosed to the app administrator.
  • Declaration that the person registering is at least 18 years old, and records of acceptance of applicable legal documents.

Physical boards

  • Board codes, edition and physical instance identifiers, registration requests, ownership, transfers and Board Associate records.
  • Private verification information, such as the intended owner's name and email, and relevant administrator decisions.

Tournaments and ratings

  • Invitations, participants, organisers, scores, Trick Shots, match results, confirmations, disputes, notes, audit records and historical results.
  • Twelve Ratings and competition history calculated from eligible approved tournaments.

Technical support and beta feedback

  • Messages you send to us, reported errors and information needed to provide, secure and troubleshoot the service.
  • Through Apple TestFlight, feedback and technical information Apple makes available to the developer, which may include device, operating system, installation and crash details.

Information may also be entered by other registered players, board owners, organisers or administrators when they invite someone to play, record a tournament or request verification.

3. How and why we use information

  • Account management: create and verify accounts, maintain sessions and manage authentication. The usual lawful basis is performance of our agreement with you.
  • Board registration and competition: verify board rights, manage invitations and tournaments, record results and calculate Twelve Ratings. The lawful basis may be performance of our agreement or our legitimate interests in operating a reliable game community.
  • Administration, disputes and security: check identity, investigate reports, preserve accurate histories, prevent misuse and protect other players' rights. We generally rely on legitimate interests or, where applicable, a legal obligation.
  • Support and beta improvement: respond to enquiries, investigate technical faults and improve the app; generally legitimate interests, with consent where specifically required.
  • Legal compliance: respond to applicable regulatory or legal requirements.

Where a processing activity depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing based on another lawful basis.

4. What other users can see

Depending on permissions and screens, other players may see a username, avatar, board-owner status, participation, competition results and Twelve Ratings. Tournament information may be accessible to authorised participants and organisers. Administrators can access additional records where their duties require it.

Private account email addresses, real-name records and board verification information are not intended for general public display. Competition history may remain after an account is deleted, subject to the limits described below.

5. Service providers

We use the following providers in connection with the service, according to their respective roles and arrangements:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and related app infrastructure.
  • Resend — delivery of account-related emails.
  • Apple TestFlight — distribution of iOS beta builds and tester feedback/diagnostics.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting this legal-information website.
  • Google Drive — restricted storage of encrypted private backups where used.

We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary for investigating security concerns or legitimate disputes. We do not sell users' personal information.

6. International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires a transfer mechanism or safeguards, we seek to use appropriate arrangements. Contact us if you want more information about our relevant providers, locations and safeguards.

7. Retention of information

We keep personal information only while reasonably necessary for its purpose and any applicable legal or accountability requirements. We apply the following retention criteria:

  • Account and sign-in details are ordinarily needed while the account exists and while a valid request or security matter is being processed.
  • Board ownership and transfer history may need to remain to establish provenance and resolve future disputes.
  • Approved competition results and ranking source history may need to remain to preserve reliable results for other participants.
  • Security, administrator and support records are reviewed according to their continuing operational or legal purpose.
  • Restricted encrypted backups may retain earlier data temporarily for recovery. Their treatment is considered separately from records in the live database.

De-identification of a displayed username is not automatically complete anonymisation of all underlying information. Where personal information remains identifiable, applicable data protection requirements continue to apply.

8. Deleting your account and personal information

How to request deletion: In the release of Twelve that offers account deletion, sign in and go to Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account. The process lets you initiate deletion of your full app account and associated personal information, not simply disable or deactivate it. You may be asked to verify your password and confirm your request.

What happens next: The designed process includes a seven-day cancellation period. Following that period, an administrator may need to resolve outstanding tournament, board-ownership or other legitimate responsibilities before completing the deletion. We aim to complete verified requests within 28 days where practicable and applicable law permits, and will inform you if further time or information is necessary. We will provide confirmation when deletion has been completed.

What is removed and what may remain: Account access and personal information no longer needed will be removed or de-identified as appropriate. Some board provenance, competition results, rating records, audit evidence or legally required records may have to be retained to protect other participants' legitimate rights or comply with the law. We will explain relevant retention when responding to a request. Removing app registration does not transfer or destroy ownership of a physical board.

Private-beta availability: The in-app deletion option is currently withheld in the first internal TestFlight build while final validation takes place. Until an updated build makes it available, contact thegameoftwelve@gmail.com to exercise your privacy rights or raise a deletion request. This interim email route does not replace the intended in-app deletion process for broader distribution.

For other privacy requests, contact the same email address. Do not send your password.

9. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to or port your personal information, depending on the circumstances and lawful basis. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will consider your objection in accordance with applicable law.

Contact thegameoftwelve@gmail.com to exercise your rights. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information, including authenticated access, database permissions, restricted administrator functions and protected backups. No service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Age requirement

To create a Twelve app account, users must declare that they are at least 18 years old. This is an account requirement, not a restriction on playing the physical tabletop game outside the app. Contact us if you believe an underage person has registered.

12. External links and testing

The app may link to Instagram and other external services; those providers have their own privacy notices. Apple independently processes information relating to TestFlight under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

13. Automated rankings

Twelve Ratings are calculated using approved competition results and the game's published eligibility and rating rules. They are game rankings, not decisions intended to produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the application and our processing practices develop. Where required, we will inform users of material changes. A date and version will identify the published notice.
