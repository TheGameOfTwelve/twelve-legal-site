These terms concern the mobile application and its services. They do not themselves form a contract for purchasing, delivering or transferring a physical Game of Twelve board. Separate arrangements may apply to physical products and events.

2. The purpose of the app

The Game of Twelve is played using a physical board, tiles and dice. The app does not replace physical play. It helps eligible users register boards, organise private one-to-one tournaments, record scores and Trick Shots, confirm results, and view Twelve Ratings. Some features depend on permissions, account state and board verification.

Features may change during private beta testing. We will try to communicate material changes that affect users.

3. Eligibility and accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create a Twelve app account, even if younger people can play the physical game.
  • You must provide accurate information when registering and maintain access to your own email account.
  • Keep login details secure. Do not share an account with another player or use someone else's identity.
  • Each person should use their own account. The administrator can verify, restrict or suspend access where justified by app security, legitimate board rights or serious misuse.

4. Board registration and ownership

Registration of a physical board in the app does not itself establish legal ownership of that board. Board identification, expected owner information, registration and transfers are subject to our verification processes.

An eligible, verified active board owner may be permitted to create private tournaments using an actively owned registered board. Board Associates and transferred board rights follow the app's established rules. The app records and manages permissions; it does not independently sell, transfer or revoke physical property.

5. Tournaments and results

Private tournaments are recorded to reflect genuine in-person play. Organisers and participants must enter accurate information and use the approved confirmations and problem-reporting processes.

  • The authorised organiser controls live scoring, subject to app rules.
  • Players may be asked to accept invitations, confirm checkpoints and confirm or report final results.
  • Administrator approval and eligibility rules determine when results affect official Twelve Ratings.
  • We may investigate disputed, incorrect or abusive records through the app's established procedures, preserving a relevant audit trail.

Please do not fabricate matches, impersonate players, manipulate ratings or create fraudulent board-ownership claims.

6. Rankings and historical records

Twelve Ratings are calculated under the app's established eligibility and rating rules. Rankings are a record of eligible competition within the Twelve system, not a guarantee of personal skill, prize entitlement or professional assessment.

Approved tournament results, board provenance and competition history may be retained for integrity, dispute resolution and the legitimate interests of other participants, even if an account is later closed, as described in the Privacy Policy.

9. Account closure and deletion

You can stop using the app and may request deletion of your app account and associated personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention needs.

In a release with the feature enabled, the intended route is Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account. This starts a full account-deletion request, rather than merely deactivating the account. You may need to verify your identity. Our designed process includes a seven-day cancellation period and administrator review of outstanding board, tournament and other responsibilities.

We aim to complete verified requests within 28 days where practicable and legally appropriate, or explain any justified delay. The process should end with confirmation. Some historical results or board provenance may be retained where permitted or required, as set out in the Privacy Policy.

First internal beta build: the in-app deletion control is temporarily hidden while the production process undergoes final validation. Until an updated build makes it available, contact thegameoftwelve@gmail.com about deletion or other privacy requests. This interim route is not presented as a substitute for the in-app control required for wider distribution.

Closing an app account does not itself transfer a physical board or undo a genuine completed match.
